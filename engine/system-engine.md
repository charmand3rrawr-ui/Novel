# The System Engine

The protagonist's private system — **the Tally** — and the rules that keep it
from eating the novel.

Three files:

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `system.json` | how the system **works** — attributes, mote tiers, sources, caps, realms, traits, professions |
| `system-status.json` | where the protagonist **is** — stats, points, residue, traits, professions, and the full absorption log |
| `system-status.md` | the readable character sheet, generated by `novel.py system sheet` |

World-level rules — realms, techniques, sorcery, bloodlines, talents — live in
[`bible/power-systems.md`](../bible/power-systems.md), and apply to everyone.
This file is only about the thing that is his alone.

## The premise

> Everything that ends sheds what it was. He alone can see the residue, and
> take it.

Not a gift, not a voice, not a menu handed down by a dead god. It is never
explained by anyone in the world, because nobody in the world knows what it is
(`LK-SYSTEM-UNEXPLAINED`). It can be theorised about. It can be hunted. It never
gets an origin story.

## The loop

```
something ends  →  it sheds motes  →  he absorbs  →  points
points          →  attributes, technique proficiency, profession build
attributes      →  realm requirements met
a scene under pressure  →  breakthrough, paid for in something that does not come back
```

## Sources — what sheds

Ten source classes are defined in `system.json → source_classes`. Three matter
more than the rest:

**`ambient_training`** — the shed effort of other people training nearby. This
is the signature source and the moral engine of the whole book. He grows on
labour that was not his, from people who will never know, and the novel's
central question — *can anything be given rather than owed?* — now has a knife
in it. He is not being given anything. He is taking, quietly, from everyone.

**`failed_craft`** — forgings, batches and inscriptions that failed. Failure
sheds more than success does, which is why he haunts workshops, and why the
professions in [`bible/professions.md`](../bible/professions.md) are not a side
quest. A Master alchemist fails four batches in ten. He is standing there for
all four.

**`ruin`** — places where something ended long ago. The richest tier, and the
most tainted. Every point from a ruin carries residue.

## The four rails

Power systems destroy long novels by inflating. Four rules stop it, and each
one refuses out loud with a reason.

### 1. Familiarity decay

Absorbing repeatedly from the same source class pays less each time:
`multiplier = decay ^ (times already absorbed this realm)`, floored at 0.15.

Grinding the same rabbits stops working after four rabbits. Finding a *new kind
of ending* is always worth more than repeating an old one — which pushes the
plot outward into new places and new dangers, exactly where you want it.

A breakthrough resets every counter. The frontier reopens, and the first thing
he does in a new realm is go back over old ground and find it rich again.

### 2. Caps

25 points per chapter soft, 60 hard, 320 per arc. Over soft, the engine warns
that the reader is starting to feel the escalation. Over hard, it refuses.

`--override` always exists and writes the exception into the log with the
chapter it happened in. The cap is not a moral rule; it is a pacing instrument,
and a deliberate spike after fifteen disciplined chapters is worth more than a
system that never says no.

### 3. Residue

Tainted sources — corpses, ruins, things killed — leave residue that the system
has no way to spend. It accumulates, and at 25 / 50 / 75 / 100 it does
something on the page:

| Residue | State | On the page |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | marked | sleep goes wrong; his sense of essence smears at the edges |
| 50 | fouled | circulation stutters under strain; he is worse in long fights |
| 75 | deviating | a deviation event is *due*, at the worst available time |
| 100 | critical | the next absorption of any kind triggers it |

Clearing it costs money, a favour, or trust: a Rank 4+ cleansing pill, a Rank 3+
cook working slowly, or rest the tempo will not give him. Residue is how the
system's convenience becomes the plot's problem.

### 4. The witness rule and who paid

`system absorb` requires `--scene` and `--who-paid`. Neither is decoration.

- **`--scene`** enforces that an absorption happened on the page. Anything that
  happened in summary did not happen.
- **`--who-paid`** enforces `LK-MOTE-COSTS`: nothing sheds essence without
  losing it. The log therefore reads as a ledger of everyone the protagonist
  has quietly taken from, and by chapter forty that document *is* the novel's
  case against him. `novel.py system log` prints it.

## Breakthroughs

Meeting the numbers makes a breakthrough **possible**. That is all. The engine
will refuse one without `--scene`, and every realm carries a cost that it debits
from the story's cost tracks in `progression.json` — body, anonymity, intimacy,
integrity — so the power ladder and the character ladder stay welded together
(`LK-BREAKTHROUGH-COSTS`, `LK-COST`).

```
$ novel.py system realm --to 2 --scene "the quay stair, bleeding, with the tide coming in"
BREAKTHROUGH — Kindling (early)
  paid on the story's cost tracks: {'body': 1, 'anonymity': 1}  → now {'body': 7, 'anonymity': 8}
  familiarity reset: every source class pays full again. The frontier reopened.
  Essence ignites. It is survivable and it is not private — someone always feels it.
```

## Attributes and the cost curve

Raising an attribute from `v` to `v+1` costs `1 + floor(v / 10)` points. Cheap
early, punishing later. Reaching 50 in one attribute costs 150 points; reaching
100 costs 550. Rare attributes — fortune and affinity — cost five times as much,
and **fortune cannot be bought at all** without an override, because in this
world luck is conserved and somebody nearby pays for it.

`novel.py system forecast` prices the next realm in both points and chapters.

## Professions, in the system

Professions are tracked here because they are a power ladder, not a hobby: every
10 proficiency earns a **build credit**, spendable on one of that profession's
attributes, chosen on the page. A smith's strength comes from the forge, and the
sheet should be able to show that it did.

Ranks 1–9 with certification pieces are in
[`bible/professions.md`](../bible/professions.md).

## What the author does not let the reader see

The status sheet is an instrument for the person writing, not a feature of the
prose. Numbers never appear on the page; strength is shown by what it does to a
room (`bible/style-guide.md`). The sheet exists so that the escalation is
*consistent* — so that what he could do in chapter 12 and what he can do in
chapter 48 are answerable questions with the same answer every time.

## Commands

```
novel.py system status                   where he stands
novel.py system forecast                 what the next realm costs, in points and chapters
novel.py system absorb ambient_training --scene "..." --who-paid "..."
novel.py system spend strength 3
novel.py system technique "Sundering Palm" --grade profound --amount 30
novel.py system profession forging --amount 12 --build strength
novel.py system realm --to 2 --scene "..."
novel.py system trait thousand-coin --scene "..."
novel.py system talent forging keen
novel.py system purge 25 --method alchemy --scene "..."
novel.py system log --chapter 12
novel.py system sheet                    regenerate the readable character sheet
```

Every one of these is validated by `novel.py validate`: point accounting,
residue state, realm requirements, profession ranks, trait states, talent
ratings, caps, and whether every absorption in the log names a scene and a payer.
