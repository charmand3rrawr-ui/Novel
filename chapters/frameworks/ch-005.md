## Failure modes

_Three specific ways THIS chapter goes wrong. Not generic craft advice._

1. the revelation is delivered as a document he reads and explains. He should understand it in one line and the reader in three, and neither should be told what it means
2. the ruin absorption reads as a power-up. It is a mistake made by a man who has just learned he was bought, and it should read as one
