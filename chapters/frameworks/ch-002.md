## Purpose

_Why this chapter exists. Test: what does the novel lose if it is cut?_

This is a **pressure** beat inside *Entrance by the Side Door*, which promises: a person with nothing gets one impossible chance. The chapter's job in that promise is to a formal evaluation that sorts people, run by an institution with its own interests.

- **Without it, the novel loses:** the only chapter where the institution states its unfairness out loud and nobody in the room finds it remarkable. Cut it and the academy becomes a villain instead of a system
- **It earns its place because:** it converts his competence from a private asset into a public record, which is what makes every later chapter dangerous

## Shape

**The Threshold Test** — A formal evaluation that sorts people, run by an institution with its own interests.

- **Opening move:** The rules are stated, and one of them is unfair in a way nobody comments on.
  - _here:_ the invigilator reads the rules aloud, including the one about the register, and the hall's silence is the sound of everyone who already has it memorised
- **Turn:** The protagonist finds the gap between the rule as written and the rule as enforced.
  - _here:_ he finds the gap: the rule forbids consulting the register, not reconstructing it, and reconstruction is the thing he is best at in the world
- **Closing move:** He passes by the gap. Someone notices the gap, not the pass.
  - _here:_ he places first. The invigilator does not congratulate him. She writes something down, and it is not a mark
- **This shape costs:** anonymity, integrity
- **Watch for:** The test is a formality the reader knows he will pass. If there is no version of the scene where he fails, there is no scene.
- **Pairs well with:** demonstration, the reckoning — consider for the next chapter.

## Failure modes

_Three specific ways THIS chapter goes wrong. Not generic craft advice._

1. the test becomes a set piece about cleverness. This chapter is about a rule that is unfair in a way nobody finds remarkable — if the reader is admiring his trick, they are not hearing the silence around the rule
2. someone in the room protests the rule. Nobody protests. That is the horror
3. The test is a formality the reader knows he will pass. If there is no version of the scene where he fails, there is no scene.
