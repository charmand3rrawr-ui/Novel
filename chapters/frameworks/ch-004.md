## Shape

**The Humiliation** — A public loss that sets the terms of everything after it.

- **Opening move:** Confidence, earned and visible.
  - _here:_ the summons, the small room, the faculty member who is kind about it. Let them be genuinely kind
- **Turn:** The loss is not from weakness but from a rule he did not know was in play.
  - _here:_ the rule is read aloud and it is not about cheating. It is about inconsistency, and he realises he has no defence that does not make it worse
- **Closing move:** He does not vow revenge. He goes and finds out what the rule was.
  - _here:_ he is held pending review. He does not vow anything. He asks for a copy of the statute, the request is granted, and that is the chapter's last line
