# Factions

Each faction gets one want, one method, one thing it will not do, and one
pressure point. If a faction cannot be summarised that way, it is not yet real.

## The Ledgerhouse

- **Wants:** to remain the house whose records everyone else honours first.
- **Method:** patronage. It finds talent early, buys it with standing, and
  never has to coerce anyone.
- **Will not:** falsify an entry. It does not need to (`LK-NO-ERASURE`).
- **Pressure point:** its authority rests on other houses' deference, which is
  a habit, not a law.
- **Faces:** a patron (A2), a handler (A2), a board that never appears on page.

## The Verge Authority

- **Wants:** to keep the chokepoint priced as infrastructure and governed as
  property.
- **Method:** metering. Air, water, spin, docking (`LK-AIR-DEBT`).
- **Will not:** allow an independent audit of life-support billing.
- **Pressure point:** 40,000 residents who were never in the plan.

## The Assembly of Strand Cross

- **Wants:** to matter.
- **Method:** procedure, publicity, the occasional principled refusal.
- **Will not:** call a general strike; it would work, and they know it.
- **Pressure point:** it is the only body that can legally void a pledge, and
  has not done so in sixty-one years.

## The Reach Settlements

- **Wants:** to be left alone with unmetered water.
- **Method:** distance, self-sufficiency, and not being worth the freight.
- **Will not:** take a charter, even a good one.
- **Pressure point:** they are only unreachable while they are unprofitable.

## The Discharged

Not an organisation. A category: people whose obligations ended and who found
that the world had no procedure for what came next. They recognise each other.
They are the novel's quiet argument that the system's edges are where its
people actually live.
