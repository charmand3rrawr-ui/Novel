# History

Only what presses on the present. History enters this file when a chapter needs
it, never before.

## The Charter (≈400 years ago)

Eleven trading houses agreed to honour each other's records. It was a
convenience. Within two generations it was the state, and the assemblies that
formally govern the chartered cities had become a committee that approves what
the houses have already decided.

## The Correction (61 years ago)

A run on standing obligations collapsed three houses. The surviving houses
absorbed their books — including their pledges — and the practice of pledging
children, which had been dying out, came back for one generation as a way for
ruined families to settle. The law was never repealed, only made shameful.

**This is the protagonist's origin.** Kestrel Vane was pledged at eleven, forty
years after the Correction, by a family that still spoke of it as tradition.

## The Verge Compact (23 years ago)

Anselm's orbital chokepoint was chartered to a subsidiary of the same interests
that hold the Ledgerhouse board. The arrangement is public and boring, which is
precisely why nobody looks at it. Arc 3 looks at it.

## The Cinder Audit (9 years ago)

An audit of the moon settlement's books found a discrepancy in life-support
billing. The auditor's report was accepted, filed, and superseded by a true
entry pointing at a supply-chain adjustment. The auditor left the profession.

Arc 4 finds them.

## Live consequences

- Pledged children of the Correction generation are now in their forties, and a
  quiet number of them are inside the houses.
- No house has issued a general discharge in living memory.
- The frontier settlements exist because of the Correction, not despite it.
